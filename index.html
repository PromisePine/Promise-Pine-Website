About

Promise Pine is a Colorado Springs based wedding videography company, capturing love stories across Colorado and beyond. We believe your wedding film should feel timeless — a beautiful reflection of light, emotion, and meaning. Each film is crafted with care, blending cinematic storytelling and authentic moments to preserve the heart of your day. At Promise Pine, we promise a keepsake you'll return to for years; a memory of elegance, warmth, and love.

Hi, I'm Matthew, the filmmaker behind Promise Pine. I've been creating films since I was eight years old. What started as homemade movies with friends and family slowly grew into a lifelong passion for storytelling. Growing up in Colorado, I developed a deep love for nature, light, and the quiet beauty found in ordinary moments. Those same inspirations now guide my work as I capture couples' stories — honest, artful, and full of feeling.
