Dillon M.

We scrambled to last minute find a videographer within our budget. Promise Pine was a lifesaver!

We found out a few weeks before our wedding that several important family members could not make it. Matthew was our videographer and not only did he say he would produce full edited videos of our ceremony and speeches, he also offered to create a 6-8 minute wedding film encapsulating the entire day. He was always displaying excitement and positivity about the wedding which was a breath of fresh air from the seriousness or stress that wedding planning can often bring out in people. During the entire process from electronic communication to in person the day of the wedding, Matthew was professional, polite, and complimentary. He never felt like he was obtrusive so we were able to enjoy our day without hinderance, and he was still ready to be where he needed to be. Exactly one month after the wedding we received all our videos. We were FLOORED. The ceremony and speeches were clean and clear, and the wedding film was AMAZING. First of all, he extended it to 10 minutes for free - he wasn't adding in extra fluff but elements that we thought really fit the video. Also, he incorporated aspects of the wedding that were most important to us, and even modeled it after movie genres and songs that we liked making it feel really personal and like a whole cinematic experience. I had a friend that was so in love with the video that even though she's not even dating anyone right now, she said she needed to have Matthew as her videographer lol! We weren't planning on having anything special for videos, but we are really happy that these are the videos we get to watch for years to come. The wedding film Matthew pieced together we will especially treasure and we are just really grateful for Promise Pine and their work.
